Why these are free

The calculators are free and always will be. They do one job well: tell you the number, once.

What they can't do is remember. If you want to track every job across a year, see product usage against what you actually bought, and watch your real margin move month to month, that needs a workbook that keeps a record — and those are what we sell.

These calculators are planning tools, not financial advice. Check the numbers against your own records before setting prices.
